About Abiso PH

One clear place to know about power interruptions before they reach you.

Know before it happens.

Abiso is a free public map of power interruptions in the Philippines, plus a simple portal utilities use to post them. It is live today for Mactan Electric Company (MECO) in the Lapu-Lapu, Cordova and Mactan area.

The problem

Outage news is scattered, easy to miss, and often unclear.

During an outage, Filipinos hunt through scattered Facebook posts to find out if it affects their street, whether it is planned, and when power is coming back. The information is hard to search, easy to miss, and often unclear.

Utilities, meanwhile, have no clean public channel and repeat the same answers by hand.

What Abiso does

One always-current map, and a portal that makes posting simple.

Anyone can open the map, no app and no login. Utilities get a guided way to post interruptions and keep an orderly record of them.

Open to everyone

One always-current map anyone can open, with no app and no login. Just one link to check what is out, where, and until when.

A page for every notice

Each notice has its own page with a plain-language reason, the exact affected area, the landmarks people recognise, and a live status.

Simple for utilities

Utilities get a guided portal to post interruptions in minutes and keep an orderly record of them, with no GIS work required.

Mission

Clear, current, and easy to find.

To make official power-interruption information clear, current, and easy to find for every Filipino household and business. Abiso's promise is to be the official source, relayed faithfully. It does not speak for a utility, it carries each utility's own advisories accurately.

Vision

One trusted place to plan your day.

Start with power, then grow into the other interruptions people plan their day around, such as water service and road closures, so there is one trusted place to know before it happens.

Where Abiso started

From a personal map of Mactan to a platform for utilities.

Abiso began as a personal project, the "MECO Feeder Map," built in 2026 to make Mactan's power advisories easier to follow. It grew into Abiso PH, a platform any Philippine electric utility can use for its own franchise area.

What we value

Trust and faithful sourcing, above all.

Faithful sourcing

Trust comes first. Abiso carries each utility's own official notices accurately, word for word with the advisory it relays.

Independence

Abiso relays each utility's own official notices and is not affiliated with a utility unless stated.

Free and open

Free and open for the public to use. Anyone can check the map, anytime, without an account.
